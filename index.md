## 🔐 What Is witness-vault?

witness-vault is a zero-install vault tool. “Zero-install” means you do not need a traditional installer or a complex setup process. You download the application, open it on Windows, and use it to create encrypted vaults that work as HTML files.

“Air-gapped” means the vault can be used without an internet connection. This is useful when you want to keep private information away from online services. You can store the vault on your computer, move it to a USB drive, or keep it in an offline folder.

“Self-decrypting HTML” means the vault file includes what it needs to unlock itself when you enter the correct password. You open the HTML file in your browser, type your password, and view the protected content. The encryption uses AES-256-GCM, a strong and widely respected encryption method, through the browser’s built-in WebCrypto technology.

In plain terms: witness-vault helps you create private vault files that stay under your control, work offline, and open with a password.
